# Ember and Frost

A two-player duel. Summon creatures, cast spells, and bring your opponent's **Life** from 20 to 0.

## Setup
- Each player picks a deck (a premade starter, or one they built) and draws **5 cards**.
- Set your **Life** counter to 20.
- The player who rolls highest on the dice roller goes first.

## Your turn
1. **Draw** one card.
2. **Gain energy**: you have energy equal to the turn number (max 6). Track it in your **Energy** counter.
3. **Play cards** whose cost you can pay:
   - **Creatures** go on your **Battlefield**.
   - **Spells** go to the **Stack** while they resolve, then to your **Graveyard**.
   - **Items** go in your **Support** row and stay there.
4. **Attack**: each creature that was on the battlefield at the start of your turn may attack. Your opponent may block with one of their creatures. Creatures deal damage equal to their power. Unblocked damage hits the opponent's Life.
5. Creatures whose damage taken reaches their health go to the Graveyard.

## Winning
Reduce your opponent's Life to 0.
