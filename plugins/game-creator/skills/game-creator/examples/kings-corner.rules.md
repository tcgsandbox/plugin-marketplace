# Kings Corner

A shedding game for 2 to 4 players using one standard 52-card deck.

## Setup
- The **Draw pile** in the centre is already shuffled and face down.
- Each player takes **7 cards** from the Draw pile into their hand.
- Turn the next 4 cards face up, one onto each **foundation** (North, South, East and West).

## Your turn
1. Take one card from the Draw pile.
2. Make as many plays as you like:
   - Play a card onto a pile if it is **one rank lower and the opposite colour** (red on black, black on red).
   - Put a **King** in an empty **corner**.
   - Move a whole foundation pile onto another pile when its bottom card fits there.
   - Play any card into an empty foundation.
3. When you can't or don't want to play more, your turn ends.

## Winning
The first player to empty their hand wins.
