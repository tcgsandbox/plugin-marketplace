# Trash

A two-player race to fill your ten spots, Ace through 10, using one standard 52-card deck. Also known as Garbage.

## The table
- **Draw pile**: shuffled and face down.
- **Discard pile**: face up.
- Each player has ten **spots** (A, 2, 3 ... 10) and a **Face-down** stack. Nobody can see the Face-down cards, not even their owner.

## Setup
Each player moves **10 cards** from the Draw pile onto their Face-down stack, without looking at them.

## Your turn
1. Take the top card of the Draw pile (or the Discard pile) into your hand.
2. If it is an Ace to 10 and that spot is still empty, put it on the spot. Then take any card from your Face-down stack into your hand. It stands for the hidden card that was in that spot, and is just as unknown. Play it the same way.
3. **Jacks are wild**: put a Jack on any empty spot and carry on.
4. Your turn ends when you hold a **Queen or King**, or a card whose spot is already filled. Put that card on the Discard pile.

## Winning
The first player to fill all ten spots wins the round.
